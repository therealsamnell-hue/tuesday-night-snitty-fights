## Summary
Luneta and Chonks, separated since events in Cauldron, reunite at the Highharvestide festival in Sathara. Silon, a Sathara native, and Talamvor, from Ulgarth, are also present.

During the festival, Chonks participates in a sausage eating contest. He is victorious. Silon attempts to guess the item inside a mystery box as part of a different game. She fails but is hit on by [[Cyrdak Daryuk]], proprietor of the [[The Sathara Ostentorium|Sathara Ostentorium]]

In the evening, all four are present outside [[The Hall of Bright Coin]] for speeches by [[First Kandara Deyarin]], who roasts workaholic tanner [[Laris Ravashki]] for taking enough time off work to show up. She also announces that [[Oligarch Skouloun Kaskar]], who was to give a speech, has taken suddenly ill and cannot attend. The Oligarch rather notoriously dislikes festivities so his "sickness" surprises no one. [[Thanadar Bahram Daryush]] then takes the stage to soberly advise caution about the night's bonfires, which rather kills the mood until Cyrdak gives a comical rendition of how Sathara raised money to build its new temple to Waukeen that ends with the news that tonight, and tonight only, he has secured Magnimaran diva [[Alishanda yr Jasmal el Mostana yi Calimport]] to perform The Harpy's Curse.

Finally it's time for the [[Asbar yn Zainab el Waukeen, Holycoin of the Hall of Bright Coin]] to speak and commence the dedication of the new temple. This replaces Sathara's previous house of worship, which burned to the ground five years ago. He reads from the Charges of Telchar, thanks the many supporters of the temple's construction in order of size of their contributions, acknowledges the faiths of Chauntea, Helm, Lathander, Savras, and Sune for their support and roles as first tenants at the rental chapels within the temple. 

He casts a thunderstone to the ground to announce the beginning of the Prayer of First Profit, at which point mayhem breaks loose as goblins rise up and attack Sathara's people, property, and pets. The party burst into action, slaying the first wave of attackers and becoming acquainted (reacquainted in Luneta and Chonks' case) during a brief lull in the mayhem. 

A wave of torch-wielding goblins then descend upon them, firing a wagon on the way and led by Goebin Gonis of Six-Seven Degrees. After those raiders are defeated, the party finds that Sathara's guard have come into action and order is slowly being restored, though fires still burn. Near to [[The White Peacock]] they come upon Magnimaran Oligarch [[Aseir yn Faruk el Pashar]] and his faithful hound, Whisperbiscuits. Whisperbiscuits is slain before the party can intervene and Aseir cowers behind barrels and watches the party dispatch the final goblins in sight.
