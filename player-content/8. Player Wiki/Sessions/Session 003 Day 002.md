## Summary
In the afternoon the party return from [[The Tickwood]] with their boar. They take it to the Rusty Dragon to be prepared for an evening feast. On returning there, Auvendra comes forward with a basket of freshly-cooked bread and [[Silon Note.png|a note]] from [[Almahra Avertin]], addressed to Silon. Silon explains to the party that Almahra is her governess. 

While the party wait for the evening meal, [[Skouloun Kaskar|Oligarch Kaskar]] bursts into the inn. He makes angry demands in Nimbralese, which no one speaks, and becomes increasingly beligerent. Silon hurries to fetch Auvendra, his daughter, and the two briefly argue in that tongue before the Oligarch switches to Tashalan to insult each member of the party in turn: Talamvor? A thuggish vagrant. Luneta? An egotist bent on displaying her archery "skills." Silon? A gender nonconforming malcontent. Chonks? _He licks his butthole in public._ Oligarch Kaskar does not know where Chonks' dog is and if he did he would not tell Chonks, as that might encourage him to remain in Sathara. He accuses the party of being reckless amateurs and insists that they should have left the matter of the [[Session 001 Day 001|goblin attack]] to skilled hands.

Ultimately the Oligarch demands that Auvendra shut the Rusty Dragon and come with him and her brother to Magnimar until matters settle in Sathara. She is unimpressed and calmly draws a large tankard of beer, sips it, then throws the rest on the Oligarch and his fine raiment. Stunned, he splutters and storms out of the tavern. Auvendra expresses her regret to the patrons that they are out of asshole stew.

Things soon settle back down and [[Nerik Voss]], gravetender at [[The Quiet Exchange]], comes by and asks after Talamvor. He bids the party meet with him in private to discuss a sensitive matter: during yesterday's chaos someone or something broke into the crypt of the Izakir, the late High Holycoin, which is also the resting place of several other departed acolytes. He brought the matter to the [[Thanadar Bahram Daryush|Thandar]], but the guard are stretched thin and the Thanadar advised him to seek Sathara's new heroes.

The party made their way to the graveyard, with Silon making a few insensitive digs at Nerik's past life as a smuggler. Nerik did not rise to the bait, professing his faith in the Morninglord and the fact that High Holycoin Izakir granted him a very favorable rate on his  atonement.

Around the crypt, which stood next to the town wall, the party found the tracks of many goblins and one larger, human-sized set. All led to the opened crypt. Heedless of any danger, Talamvor rushed forward and into the clutches of a pair of animated skeletons. He dispatched the first with a shield bash. Silon mastered the second, which Chonks had wounded, with her powers. She named it The Dark Vessel of Our Memories and had it stand aside while the party searched the crypt.

Most obviously, the sarcophagus containing the former High Holycoin was broken open and the body missing from it. Only a burial shroud remained. Two of the smaller crypts had also been broken open, the final resting places of deceased acolytes. Discarded in a corner, the party found a robe. It was largely intact, though two patches were torn from it. A more thorough search found the partially-burned remains of two swatches of fabric that match the robe. The party discussed what this could all mean and determined that the skeletons were likely animated by use of the robe, which radiates waning necromancy.

Nerik came forward to inquire as to what the party found and was rather disturbed to see The Dark Vessel of Our Memories standing there. He asked that the acolyte be returned to his rest and await a new dawn. Talamvor agreed with him and Silon permitted the paladin to dispatch the skeleton.

Thereafter, the party obliged Nerik's request to report to the Thanadar. He was very concerned with the tidings they brought and mused that perhaps the goblin attack was a distraction to cover the theft of the High Holycoin's body for some dark purpose. He asked that the party keep the affair quiet, as Sathara has had enough public distress to last it some time.

On returning to the Rusty Dragon, the partyfound [[Shalene Vinder|Shalene]] waiting eagerly for Chonks. And the boar is roasted and ready.
