## Summary

Having helped save Sathara from the goblin attack, Luneta, Chonks, Tallamvor, and Silon are heroes. The first to thank them is [[Aseir yn Faruk el Pashar]], an oligarch visiting from Magnimar. He praises their martial skill, particularly Luneta's, and promises them that he will better express his gratitude when he's had time to recover from his mostly superficial injuries. He'll be in Sathara for at least a few more days, staying at [[The Rusty Dragon]]. As he departs, Silon warns him to watch his drinks.

In the morning, Luneta receives a formal missive from Aseir. It praises her skill at archery and inviting her and her companions to join him on a boar hunt in the nearby [[The Tickwood|Tickwood]]. He promises their every comfort will be seen to. It's simply a matter of calling at the Rusty Dragon to come along.

The party agree to go hunting, with Luneta very eager to show off. As they approach the Rusty Dragon, [[Auvendra Kaskar]] spots them and rushes out to give them free drinks in thanks for their work saving the town. Chonks chats her up, inquiring about her interest in furries. Auvendra responds affirmatively and as they speak, he and Luneta spot [[Shalene Vinder]] watching Chonks from afar. She seems very interested in him.

On recognizing her interest, Chonks leaps into action and across the common room. She presents him with a packet of very potent catnip, Halruaan High, and informs him that they may have still stronger stuff at [[Vinder's Emporium|the family store]]...though he should also know that its basement is simply infested with delicious-looking mice and rats. Chonks greens out consuming the catnip with a feline regard for public dignity.

Before Shalene and Chonks can converse further, Aseir emerges with his porters in tow. He has ten, including one just for carrying the alcohol. They bring with them a substantial baggage train including special board that he uses to cross streams, dismounting for the act. Aseir is too sophisticated and wealthy a man to splash his horse's hooves in water, after all. The Tickwood is a mere hour and a half from Sathara, nowhere near distant enough to justify such an outlay.

At noon the party stops for a meal, with the porters unrolling a fine carpet, assembling fancy field chairs, and a table which they cover with an embroidered cloth. The Magnimaran oligarch dines well, with numerous dishes. Some were even kept warm by coals stored in special compartments in their base. The dishes include sausage, which Chonks has a strong interest in.

Not long after dining, a boar emerges from the underbrush. It roars ferociously...and Silon animates its shadow to hold it in place. Feeling sporting, Talamvor hurls two javelins at it. Chonks deploys his fiery shiruken with more success as Silon lays curses upon the increasingly helpless animal. Tallamvor and Chonks slay it and receive the applause of Aseir for the job well done. 

Their game secured, the hunting party aims to return to Sathara where they can feast at the Rusty Dragon.
