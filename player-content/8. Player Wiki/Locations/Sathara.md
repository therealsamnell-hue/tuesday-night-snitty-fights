# Sathara

> [!abstract] Settlement Profile  
> **Type:** Independent town under Magnimar's suzerainty  
> **Population:**   1,240 
> **Demographics:**  1,116 humans—760 Tashalans (61.3%), 190 Calishites (15.3%), 110 Shaarans (8.9%), 53 Chultans (4.3%), 3 Nimbralese (0.2%); 35 halflings (2.8%), 25 elves (2.0%), 20 dwarves (1.6%), 12 gnomes (1.0%), 22 half-elves (1.8%), 10 half-orcs (0.8%)
> **Government:**  Conventional (First and Council of Oligarchs)
> **Authority Figures:**  **Kandara Deyarin,** First of Sathara; **Bahram Daryush**, Thanadar; **Asbar yn Zainab el Waukeen,** Holycoin of the Hall of Bright Coin; **Mehmen Saravani,** Oligarch; **Haseid Varashan,** Oligarch; **Skouloun Kaskar,** Oligarch
> **Primary Industries:**  Lumber, viticulture, light shipbuilding, glassmaking
> **Notable Exports:** Wine, Glassworks

## Overview

Sathara is a small port town which, having the only good harbor for a significant span of the coastline, serves as the local commercial hub to the local farmlands.

## Description

Sathara stands on a sandy peninsula jutting out into its bay, walled on its landward approach. The Sea Light, a ruin of unknown provenance, stands just seaward of the town. The town is mostly not far above sea level at high tide and during powerful storms the most exposed streets often flood. The Oligarchs removed themselves to manors just south of the town and on higher ground many years ago, whence they might satisfy themselves by literally gazing down upon their lessers.

## History

Sathara was founded by a consortium of mercantile interests originally based in Magnimar, some forty years ago. The site is widely thought to have held a settlement prior to the Rage of Wizards, which devastated the region two centuries past, but no traces of that town have been found.

## Government

The town is governed by the First and Council of Oligarchs. The Council meets infrequently and often informally, leaving the day to day administration to First Deyarin. The First is well-liked by most in Sathara. The Council is a seven-member body in which the four founding families possess hereditary seats. The others are elected by a vote of those who own property of adequate taxed value, or who pay a poll tax of equivalent amount at the time of election. Sathara has almost always elected the sitting Holycoin of Waukeen to one of these seats.

## Law and Order

Thanadar Daryush commands eleven full-time guards to preserve public order. In times of crisis, he would also have the command of Sathara's fifty-strong militia.

## Religion

A slim majority of Satharans principally revere Waukeen, with the Merchant's Friend growing in popularity as one's purse swells. As such, she has long had the lone proper temple: The Hall of Bright Coin. Other faiths prominent in the town are those of Chauntea, Helm, Lathander, Savras, and Sune. With the reconstruction of the Hall of Bright Coin, the edifice was expanded to include chapel space for lease and each boasts a modest place for worship within the opulent halls.

## Places of Interest

### Districts and Neighborhoods

Hightown, resting on a low rise overlooking the city, is home to Sathara's affluent and most of its newer construction. Lowtown is closer to sea level, with flooding occasionally reaching several streets inland. Denser and older, Lowtown houses most of the town's commercial activity and government buildings.

The oligarch families separate themselves even from Hightown, maintaining manses on a hill opposite Sathara's harbor. They long secured legal title to all the lands nearby to preserve their exclusivity.
