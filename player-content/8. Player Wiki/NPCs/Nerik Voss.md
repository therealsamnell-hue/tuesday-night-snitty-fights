## Overview

Nerik is the meticulous, pious turnskull and gravewarden at [[The Quiet Exchange|the Quiet Exchange]]. His twisted spine gives him a somewhat sinister looking gait. He keeps the Quiet Exchange very clean and in good order virtually single-handedly.

## Background

Nerik is a former smuggler. Holycoin Tavani took pity on him when he was shipwrecked near to Sathara, granting him very favorable rates on atonement for his crimes against commerce. Such mercy moved Nerik, on the completion of his atonement, to take up the faith of Lathander. He views his work now as part of that faith, ensuring the dead are brought gently along to their new beginnings rather than be imprisoned forever in sterile undeath or rise as unquiet things that hate life.
