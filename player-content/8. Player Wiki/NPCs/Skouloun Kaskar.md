## Overview

Oligarch Skouloun is a retiring sort who dislikes festivities and large gatherings of people. He largely keeps to himself and tends to his business interests and his role on the Council. Though quite wealthy, perhaps the richest man in Sathara, he prefers a quieter style of ostentation than is common among his peers.

## Background

The Kaskars hail from Nimbral and constitute Sathara's lone family of Nimbralese origin, exiles from that distant, strange island for reasons they do not care to discuss. The Oligarch maintains many Nimbralese traditions, though he has never set foot on the island himself. The fact that the Oligarch's son Vaelthir is half-elven and both he and the late Oligarchess Skouloun are full-blooded humans is not one he appreciates having noted.
