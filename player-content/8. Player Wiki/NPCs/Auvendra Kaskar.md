## Overview

Auvendra is the vivacious, no-nonsense owner of [[The Rusty Dragon]], which she bought with proceeds from her adventuring career. It's the only place this side of Tashluta where one can get Nimbralese cuisine, which is very popular with most of Sathara. She's just as skilled at music as in the kitchen and often takes breaks from cooking to make brief performances.

## Background

Auvendra split from her family some five years ago and embarked on a life of adventure. Her decision on returning a year later to buy and operate an inn scandalized her father still further. She very loudly does not care what her father thinks of the whole affair and refuses to speak of why she quit the adventuring life.
